# Fotostock Workshop — Privacy Policy / Privātuma politika

Last updated / Atjaunots: 2026-11-02

## English

Fotostock Workshop (Vārdotājs, Analītiķis and related tools) are desktop programs that run on the user's own computer.

- **No servers of ours.** The programs do not send your data to the developer. There is no account with us, no tracking and no analytics.
- **Google account (optional).** If you connect your Google account, the program uses the Google Drive permission `drive.file` only. It can see and change **only the files it created itself** (for example backups, settings and manifests in the folder `Fotostokeris/` in your Google Drive). It cannot see your other Drive files, your photos or your email.
- **What is stored in your Google Drive:** program backups, settings and data needed to continue work on another computer. Your photo archive is never uploaded.
- **Sign-in tokens** are stored only in your computer's secure storage (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain), never in files or online.
- **AI services** (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and others) are used only when you connect them yourself; photos and texts are sent to the service you chose, under that service's own privacy terms.
- **Disconnecting:** you can disconnect the Google account at any time in the program (Settings → Connections) or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files the program created stay in your Drive until you delete them.
- **Use of Google user data** complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Contact: fotostockworkshop@gmail.com

## Latviski

Fotostock Workshop (Vārdotājs, Analītiķis un saistītie rīki) ir datorprogrammas, kas darbojas lietotāja paša datorā.

- **Mums nav savu serveru.** Programmas nesūta tavus datus izstrādātājam. Nav konta pie mums, nav izsekošanas un statistikas vākšanas.
- **Google konts (neobligāts).** Ja pieslēdz Google kontu, programma izmanto tikai Google Drive atļauju `drive.file`: tā redz un maina **tikai pašas izveidotos failus** (rezerves kopijas, iestatījumus un manifestus mapē `Fotostokeris/` tavā Google Drive). Tā neredz citus tavus Drive failus, bildes vai e-pastu.
- **Kas glabājas tavā Google Drive:** programmas rezerves kopijas, iestatījumi un dati, kas vajadzīgi, lai darbu turpinātu citā datorā. Bilžu arhīvs netiek augšupielādēts.
- **Pieslēguma atslēgas** glabājas tikai datora drošajā glabātuvē (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain), nekad failos vai internetā.
- **AI pakalpojumi** (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini u.c.) tiek lietoti tikai tad, ja tos pieslēdz pats; bildes un teksti tiek sūtīti izvēlētajam pakalpojumam saskaņā ar tā noteikumiem.
- **Atslēgšana:** Google kontu var atslēgt jebkurā brīdī programmā (Iestatījumi → Pieslēgumi) vai https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Programmas izveidotie faili paliek tavā Drive, līdz tos izdzēs.
- Google lietotāju datu lietošana atbilst Google API Services User Data Policy, ieskaitot Limited Use prasības. Dati netiek pārdoti, nodoti trešajām pusēm vai izmantoti reklāmai.

Kontakts: fotostockworkshop@gmail.com
